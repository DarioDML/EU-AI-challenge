    Prepare now, in normal times
    • Know your way to higher ground and to the building in this plan; walk it once.
    • Install an official warning app and keep your phone charged; keep a power bank.
    • Keep a small bag ready: ID and important papers in a waterproof bag, medicines, glasses, cash, phone numbers on paper.
    • Store drinking water and food for a few days, a torch and a battery radio.
    • Agree with family and neighbours who helps whom (children, older people, pets).
    • Move valuables and important papers above ground floor if your area flooded in 2021.
